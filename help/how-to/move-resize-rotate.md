# Move, resize and rotate

## By dragging

| Drag | Does |
|---|---|
| The object | Move |
| A corner handle | Resize |
| An edge handle | Stretch one way |
| The handle above | Rotate |
| **Alt** + corner handle | Skew |

**Arrow keys** nudge 1 mm. Hold **Shift** for 10 mm, **Alt** for 0.1 mm.

## By typing exact numbers

1. Select the object.
2. In **Properties → Transform**, type the centre, width, height or angle.

Numbers are positions, not moves. Typing `75` in CX puts the centre **at** X 75.

## Flip

Click **Mirror X** or **Mirror Y** in Properties.

## Watch out

Rotating or stretching a shape turns it into a plain path. Its settings (like a circle's
radius) are lost. Moving or resizing evenly keeps them.
