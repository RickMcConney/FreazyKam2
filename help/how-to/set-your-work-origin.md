# Set your work origin (X0 Y0 Z0)

Choose where zero sits on the stock. It must match where you zero the machine.

1. Click **Setup**.
2. Under **Work Origin**, click the spot for **X0 Y0**. Bottom-left is the usual choice.
3. Pick **Z Origin**:

   | Choose | When you zero the bit on |
   |---|---|
   | **Top of stock** (usual) | The top of the material |
   | **Bottom of stock** | The table or spoilboard |

4. Click **×**.

**Check it:** when you export, the review repeats your origin. Read it before you cut.
