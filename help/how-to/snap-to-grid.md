# Snap to the grid and to other parts

Turn snapping on and off with the **magnet** in the toolbar, or press **S**. While it's on,
the status bar shows **SNAP**.

With snapping on, a part you drag or place:

- lines up with the edges, centres and corners of other parts and of the stock, with a
  guide line to show it;
- spaces itself evenly between neighbours;
- otherwise lands on the grid.

The grid square changes as you zoom. The status bar shows its size as **Grid**.

Turn snapping off to place a part exactly where the mouse is.
