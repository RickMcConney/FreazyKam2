# Add or remove paths from an operation

No need to start over if you missed a hole.

1. Click the operation's row in **Paths → Toolpaths** (or its chip) to open its form.
2. **Shift**-click paths on the canvas to add or remove them.
3. Check the chips at the bottom of the form. Added ones are marked; removed ones are struck
   through.
4. Click **Regenerate**.

A shape added inside a pocket becomes an island of that pocket.

## Change a setting

1. Open the operation the same way.
2. Change the setting.
3. Click **Regenerate Toolpath**.

## Amber ring on an operation?

Something it depends on changed. Open it and click **Regenerate Toolpath**.
