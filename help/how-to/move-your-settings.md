# Move your settings to another computer

Your machine settings, tools, post-processors and form defaults live in your browser. Save
them to a file to move or back them up.

## Save

1. Click **Setup**.
2. Under **Settings File**, click **Export**.

You get a `.fkset` file.

## Load

1. On the other computer, click **Setup → Settings File → Import…**.
2. Tick the parts you want.
3. For your own backup, tick **Machine**, **stock defaults**, **form values**, **Machine tab**
   and **interface** too. Leave them unticked for someone else's file.
4. To avoid duplicate tools, set **Tools** to **Replace**.
5. Click **Import**.

Click **Export a backup** first if offered. Settings can't be undone.

## Reset the machine settings

**Setup → Restore default machine settings…** puts machine limits and feeds back. Your tools
and stock stay.
