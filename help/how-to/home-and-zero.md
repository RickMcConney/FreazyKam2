# Home and zero

**Run** won't start until X, Y and Z are zeroed on the stock.

## Home (if you have limit switches)

Under **Home & Zero**, click **Home → All**.

No limit switches? Untick **Limit switches** in the section header.

## Zero

1. Read the line under the buttons. It says **where** to zero (from Setup).
2. [Jog](jog-the-machine.md) the bit to that corner of the stock.
3. Click **Zero → X** and **Zero → Y**.
4. Jog Z down until the bit touches the surface, or [use a touch plate](probe-z.md).
5. Click **Zero → Z**.

A **✓** appears beside each zeroed axis.

## Zeros cleared?

Disconnecting, disabling motors or an alarm clears the ✓ marks. Home that axis again. If
nothing changed, the ✓ comes back. Otherwise zero again.

**Changed the bit?** Always zero Z again.

## Go back to zero

Click **Go → Safe Z, then X0 Y0**.
