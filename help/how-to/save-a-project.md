# Save and reopen a project

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Save | **Ctrl+S** |
| Open | **Ctrl+O** |
| New | **Ctrl+N** |

Projects save as `.fkam` files on your computer. They keep everything: shapes stay
editable, operations keep their settings.

Browser closed before you saved? Reopen the app. It offers to **restore unsaved work**.

G-code and SVG exports can't be reopened for editing. Always keep the `.fkam`.
