# Zoom and move around the canvas

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Zoom | Scroll the mouse wheel. It zooms on the cursor |
| Pan | Drag with the middle mouse button, or hold **Space** and drag |

## Zoom buttons

The buttons at the bottom right of the canvas:

- **Zoom in** and **zoom out** step the zoom. With something selected they zoom on it,
  otherwise on the middle of the view. Handy on a trackpad, where pinching over the
  canvas is awkward.
- The **arrows** between them fit the whole stock on screen.

## See parts at real size

Click **1:1** to show parts at their real size on your screen, so you can hold a part
or a drawing up against it.

It needs your screen measured once:

1. Click **Calibrate screen** in the status bar.
2. Hold a bank card (or any ID-sized card) flat against the screen, its left edge on the
   line.
3. Drag the slider until the outline is exactly as long as the card.

Each display is measured separately. Once it's done, the status bar shows the zoom as a
**Scale** against real size.
