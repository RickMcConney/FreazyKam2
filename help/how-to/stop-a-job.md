# Pause, stop or end a job early

| To | Do | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pause | **Pause** | Slows and holds. Spindle keeps turning |
| Carry on | **Resume** | Continues |
| End early (safe) | **Pause**, wait, then **Cancel job** | Stops, keeps zero, lifts Z |
| Emergency | Red **STOP** | Stops now |

**Pause then Cancel** is the best way to end early. It keeps your zeros.

**■ Stop** in the job box resets the controller. The machine alarms and may lose position.
Click **Unlock** and check the machine before continuing.

## Safety

STOP needs WiFi. Keep a power switch for the machine **and** the router within reach.
The app can't turn a trim router off.

**Stopped part way, or broke a bit?** [Restart from where it stopped](resume-a-job.md).
