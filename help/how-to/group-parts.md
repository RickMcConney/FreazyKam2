# Group parts together

1. Select the parts.
2. Press **Ctrl+G**.

They now move, resize and delete as one.

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Ungroup | Select the group, press **Ctrl+Shift+G** |
| Select one part inside | **Alt**-click it |

Groups can hold other groups. Ungrouping removes one level at a time.
