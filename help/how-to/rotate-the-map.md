# Turn the map to match where you sit

1. Click **Rotate** on the **Go to** header until it matches your seat:

   | You sit | Rotate |
   |---|---|
   | In front | 0° |
   | At the right end (+X) | 90° |
   | Behind | 180° |
   | At the left end (−X) | 270° |

2. Test: with the router off, jog **X+** a little. The marker should move the same way as
   the bit.

The jog arrows turn with the map. The machine and G-code are not changed.

**Related:** [Map reference](../reference/machine-tab.md#map-rotation)
