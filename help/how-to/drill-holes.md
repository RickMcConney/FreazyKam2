# Drill holes

## With a drill bit (Peck at Points)

1. Click the hole circles in the order you want them drilled.
2. Under **CAM Operations**, click **Drill**.
3. Under **Mode**, pick **Peck at Points**.
4. Pick a drill as the **Tool**.
5. Set **Depth**.
6. Click **Generate Toolpath**.

No circles? Click on the canvas to place points. A click near a circle uses its centre.

Peck lists every tool. Pick a drill: an end mill must be centre-cutting, and a ball nose or
taper leaves a rounded bottom. The form warns you.

## With an end mill (Helical (Circle))

Makes any size hole by spiralling down.

1. Select the hole circles.
2. Click **Drill**, then pick **Helical (Circle)** under **Mode**.
3. Pick an end mill smaller than the hole.
4. Set **Depth** and generate.

The form lists each hole's Ø and the toolpath Ø.

## Hole in a pocket floor

Set **Start** to that pocket's floor, and **Depth** to the hole's own depth.
See [Cut into the floor of a pocket](cut-into-a-pocket-floor.md).
