# Set up your stock

Tell the app what material is on your table.

1. Click the **Setup** tab in the sidebar.
2. Type **Width (X)**, **Height (Y)** and **Thickness (Z)** in mm (or inches, if the toolbar
   is set to inches).
3. Pick the **Material**. This sets the automatic feeds and speeds.
4. Click **×**.

![The Setup panel](../../docs/images/01-setup-stock.png)

**Tip:** measure the thickness. Cut-through depths and the simulation depend on it.
