# Stop parts moving by accident (Lock dragging)

Turn on the **lock** in the toolbar, or press **L**, when you keep nudging parts out of
place while selecting them. It stays on until you turn it off, even after a reload.

While it's on:

- A click on a part still **selects** it, but dragging does not move it.
- Dragging on a part draws a **selection box** instead, which is handy on a crowded sheet.
- The resize, rotate and skew **handles** are hidden.

You can still move a part on purpose:

- **Shift-drag** it.
- Nudge it with the **arrow keys**.
- Type new numbers in the **Properties** panel.
