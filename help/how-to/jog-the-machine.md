# Jog the machine

1. Pick an **XY step** under the pad (0.1, 1, 10 or 50 mm).
2. Click an arrow. Each arrow is labelled with its axis (**X+**, **Y−**…).
3. For Z, pick a step with **−** / **+** and click **Z+** or **Z−**.

The centre **⊗** cancels a move.

## Click to go

Once X and Y are zeroed, click anywhere on the map to send the tool there. Z lifts first
if needed.

## Orange arrows

The bit is below the stock surface, or the next Z step would go below it. Nothing is
blocked. It's a reminder.

## Arrows go the wrong way?

[Turn the map to match where you sit](rotate-the-map.md).
