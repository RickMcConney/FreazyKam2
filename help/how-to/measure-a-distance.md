# Measure a distance

Measure between any two points on the canvas.

1. Click the **ruler** in the toolbar, or press **M**.
2. Click the first point.
3. Click the second point.

The readout gives the distance, how far across (ΔX) and up (ΔY), and the angle.

- A click near a part's **corner**, the **middle of an edge** or its **centre** lands
  exactly on it.
- Hold **Shift** for the second point to keep the line to 15° steps.
- Click again to start a new measurement.
- Press **Esc** to clear the line. Press it again to put the tool down.
