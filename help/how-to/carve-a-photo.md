# Carve a photo

**Photo V-Carve** cuts a photo as lines of V-grooves. Dark areas cut deeper and wider.
Fill with paint and sand the face to reveal the image.

1. Under **CAM Operations**, click **Photo V-Carve**.
2. Pick the **Image**.
3. Pick a **V-bit**.
4. Set **Depth at White** and **Depth at Black**.
5. Check the line count the form shows.
6. Click **Generate Toolpath**.

## More detail or more contrast?

| Want | Do |
|---|---|
| More detail | Make it **shallower** (more, finer lines) |
| More contrast | Make it **deeper** (fewer, wider lines) |

Line spacing is set by the depth, not a separate setting.
